Result Slips

Fill in the event details, paste the round's pairing list, and the slips are generated automatically with each board's table number, round and player names. Print 16 slips per A4 page and cut along the dashed lines.

1. Event details

2. Pairing list

The first Name column is White and the second is Black. The Bo. / Board / Table column gives the table number; without one, tables are numbered in order. A table with White and Black columns also works. Byes are skipped. Everything is processed in your browser.

3. Preview and print

On each slip the arbiter ticks the I square for an illegal move and the W square for a warning, three squares per player.

TOURNAMENT TOOLKIT · Pairings are processed in your browser and are not uploaded or stored. Check the printed slips before the round.
